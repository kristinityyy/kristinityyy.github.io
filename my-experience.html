My Experience

As a junior UX/UI designer, I’ve built hands-on experience through real-world projects that allowed me to explore the full design process—from initial research to final delivery. My work has focused on creating functional, user-friendly designs with an emphasis on clean layouts, intuitive navigation, and a clear visual language.

I’ve designed both mobile and web interfaces, including a website for a Finnish cleaning company and a mobile application for a startup focused on substitute teacher placement. In both cases, I was responsible for tasks such as wireframing, user flows, UI design, prototyping, and conducting basic user testing. These projects strengthened my ability to think both creatively and strategically when solving design problems.

I enjoy working closely with users and stakeholders to understand their needs and turn those into accessible, responsive digital experiences. I’m familiar with tools like Figma, Adobe XD, and basic front-end concepts, and I’m always open to learning new technologies and workflows.

Every project I’ve worked on has helped me grow not only as a designer but also as a communicator and problem-solver. I’m now excited to take the next step by contributing to new projects, collaborating with creative teams, and continuing to learn through real design challenges.
